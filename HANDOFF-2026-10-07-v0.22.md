## Hidden causes: checked and ruled out

- **Background re-check cost.** Reports show `seg_bgChoice` 54–69 ms, but
  that is the median of frames where it ran: 6–10% of frames, 2–6% of total
  time. Not a problem.
- **Duplicates lying on top of each other.** IMG_3605: 60 entries, 54
  position clusters (< 0.7 unit apart), only 6 pairs share a spot. Entry
  clean-up is not the main issue; see "excess entries" below.

## Hidden causes: found, not yet fixed (highest value first)

1. **The quality floor turns close-up reads into "far" reads.**
   `PH.isCloseRead` needs `meanSide ≥ 120` *and* `quality.q ≥ 0.25`. On
   IMG_3605, 34 of 138 reads called "far" were ≥ 120 px and failed only the
   quality floor (q 0.13–0.20); 18 entries got such reads, 9 of them have
   0–1 accepted close reads (i.e. they stay unchecked). On IMG_3593: 12 and
   2. A far read is "never mixed in" (`engine.js`, the `!nc` branch), so
   these views do nothing.
   Hypothesis to test: let a quality-failed close-size read count as
   *agreement* (not as the stored shape) when its shape agrees with the
   stored close read — two independent views agreeing is the evidence
   checking wants. Low-q reads alone are unreliable (agreement 23% when the
   lower q < 0.5), so never store them.
   How to measure: extend the `READSTUDY` hook in `test/real-50.js` to also
   compare the quality-failed close-size reads with the stored read
   (`PH.samePiece`/`PH.shapeAgree`) and, on IMG_3593, mark right/wrong per
   key. If most agreeing ones are right, implement in the `!nc` branch and
   in `noteCloseAgree`; compare 3 runs each on IMG_3605, CPU clock.
2. **Excess entries on the counter.** 54 clusters for 38 real pieces; 29
   unchecked entries stand alone, 12 far-only, 11 with ≤ 3 sightings. Which
   are drifted copies of real pieces and which are junk (shadow blobs,
   fragments) can't be told without ground truth.
   Do first: **build an answer key for IMG_3605** with
   `tools/answer-key-build.js` (usage in its header; the key format is
   `test/fixtures/v3593/answer.json`; the overview frames are at 72–80 s
   and the end). Then `real-50.js 3605` reports one-to-one, doubles,
   identity, like IMG_3593. Without it, every counter-video change is judged
   on a noisy count (23–31 checked across runs of the same code).
3. **Test timing does not match the phone.** Phone reports: `seg` 74–116
   ms, `total` 145–212 ms a frame at 3.5–6 fps, against `budgetMs` 45; the
   tests run Node's non-SIMD OpenCV on a CPU clock. When a frame is over
   budget the read loop allows 2 reads + at most 1 confirmation
   (`runQueue`), so the phone confirms fewer pieces per frame than the
   tests do. Check cheaply from report `history` (`t1` per frame) and from
   `tracker`/`timings`; if confirmed, make `cpu-clock.js` take a scale
   factor (e.g. `CLOCK=cpu:2.5`) and judge the videos at phone speed too.
4. **Worker-camera turn is unverified on the phone.** If `camProbeTurn`
   ever picks the wrong turn (90 vs 270), every mark lands wrong. The
   fallback (bitmaps after 8 undecided probes) exists. Cheap hardening
   without a phone: re-probe every ~60 frames and drop to bitmaps if the
   chosen turn stops winning; `test/cam-turn.js` covers the maths.
5. **Joined pairs are not cut apart.** 4 pieces inside the pairs placed
   joined on purpose (IMG_3621, IMG_3622) never read. Assembled parts are
   deliberately not catalogued, so this needs a seam-following cut for
   *small* clumps (2 pieces, a straight dark seam: `PH.innerSeam` already
   detects seams). Target in `real-joins.js`: 16 → 20 of 21 pieces.
6. **IMG_3627's third piece** merges with its lamp shadow along one side
   (plan Batch 4: shadow-aware colour distance, outline snapped to the
   intensity edge). Measure on the known-weak line.
7. **Find mode (plan Batch 3)**: "Strong match 70%" (verdict from one side,
   probability the min of both — `scanPairs`), and a `merged` blob offered
   as a partner (IMG_3630). Both fixable with synthetic tests, no phone.
8. Unverified on the phone, nothing to do without one: v0.21.3 pockets,
   v0.21.4 edge-to-edge arrows, v0.22.2 hold-still hint (`blurHint` in
   reports), v0.22.0 camera turn (`cameraPath`, `camProbe`).

## Tools added this session

- `test/real-50.js`: `CLOCK=cpu` (budgets on process CPU time);
  `READSTUDY=<file.json>` writes every close read (frame, entry, sharpness,
  q, code, the stored read it was compared with and whether they agreed;
  with a key, right/wrong), the frames' sharpness, every read attempt
  (`jobs`), per-frame sightings (`seen`), final entries; `PHSET='{"X":1}'`
  overrides `PH` constants for experiments. Prints *first checked piece
  at* and *checked at 30 s*.
- `test/cpu-clock.js`: preload (`node -r ./test/cpu-clock.js …`).
  Step clock for determinism: a preload with
  `let t = 0; performance.now = () => (t += 0.02);`.
- A/B against an old version: `git worktree add --detach <scratch>/base
  <commit>`, junction `node_modules` into it, run with
  `VISION=<base>/js/vision`. Removing the worktree fails on read-only files
  under `.git/worktrees/<name>`: clear attributes, then delete.
- Scratch probes (gitignored, `test/out/`): `bgprobe.js` (every background
  model per photo → pieces read), `segdraw.js <IMG>` (segmentation drawn,
  `UNIT=` forces a piece size, `DBG=1`), `fitlog.js` (photo search trials),
  `segtime.js` (segmentation stage times), `radius.js`, `probe3627.js`.
- `test/real-joins.js`: `at:` positions for turned layouts; known-weak
  photos reported as `WEAK`, with a floor on pieces read (16 of 21).
