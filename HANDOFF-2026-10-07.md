## Useful commands
- `npm test` is the full suite (includes `clump-split.js 150` and `pockets.js`). `npm run test:real` runs the owner's videos and photos.
- `python tools/report-summary.py reports/<report>.json` summarises a phone report.
- `node test/report-replay.js <report.json> --draw` replays a report frame through segmentation.
- `node tools/replay-video.js <video> <box.jpg> <seconds> <pieces> <cols>x<rows>` replays a video in headless Edge.
- `node test/real-50.js 3609` with env `BG=1 BGLOG=1 FORCEBG='[30,80]' VOTE=1 DETSTATS=1 TILT=0` gives glass-video diagnostics.
- Scratch probes from this session are in `test/out/` (gitignored): `bgx.js`, `edgedraw.js`, `snap1.js`, `snap2.js`, `p1.js`, `p2.js`.
